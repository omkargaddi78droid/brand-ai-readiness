This clinic treats hypertension, diabetes, and thyroid disorders. Our cardiologists specialize in arrhythmia and atherosclerosis. We also diagnose autoimmune conditions like lupus and rheumatoid arthritis.

Patients receive a full metabolic panel at intake. This checks glucose, creatinine, and electrolyte levels. We also test for hyperlipidemia and hypothyroidism.

Anticoagulant therapy requires regular monitoring. We check prothrombin time and INR weekly. Dosage adjustments happen based on these results.

Our pharmacology team reviews every prescription. They check for contraindications and drug interactions. Nephrotoxicity and hepatotoxicity are flagged automatically.

Immunosuppressant regimens follow strict protocols. Transplant patients get tacrolimus or cyclosporine. Levels are checked monthly to avoid nephrotoxicity.

Endocrinology consults cover thyroid and adrenal disorders. We test TSH, T3, and T4 levels. Cortisol testing happens for suspected adrenal insufficiency.

Oncology patients undergo chemotherapy under close supervision. Neutropenia is monitored via weekly blood counts. Antiemetics are prescribed to manage nausea.

Our radiology suite offers MRI, CT, and ultrasound. Contrast agents require a kidney function check first. Gadolinium is avoided in patients with renal impairment.

Physical therapy addresses musculoskeletal injuries. We treat tendinitis, bursitis, and rotator cuff tears. Rehabilitation plans are customized per patient.

Every department follows evidence-based clinical guidelines. Documentation is thorough and standardized. Quality metrics are reviewed quarterly by our compliance team.

Our laboratory runs standard hematology panels. We check hemoglobin, platelets, and white cell counts. Abnormal results trigger a physician callback same day.

Dermatology handles eczema, psoriasis, and dermatitis cases. Biopsies are read by our pathology team. Results are usually ready within a week.

Gastroenterology covers colonoscopy and endoscopy procedures. We screen for polyps and ulcers routinely. Sedation is managed by our anesthesia team.

Pediatrics follows standard immunization schedules. We track growth curves at every visit. Developmental screening happens at key milestones.

Ophthalmology handles cataract and glaucoma cases. We measure intraocular pressure at each visit. Surgical referrals go out within two weeks.
