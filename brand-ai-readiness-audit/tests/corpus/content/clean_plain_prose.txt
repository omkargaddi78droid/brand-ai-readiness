Our team ships new features every two weeks. Each release goes through a short review before it goes live. We post the full changelog on our blog.

Signing up takes less than a minute. Just enter your email and pick a password. You can start using the app right away.

Support is open Monday through Friday. Send us a message and we will reply within a day. Most questions get answered the same day.

We back up your data every night. Backups are kept for thirty days. You can restore an older version at any time from your account settings.

The mobile app works on both phones and tablets. It syncs with the web app automatically. Any change you make shows up everywhere within seconds.

Billing is simple and clear. You pick a plan and pay once a month. You can switch plans or cancel at any time from your account page.

Our help center has short guides for every feature. Each guide has screenshots and step by step instructions. Most users find what they need in under a minute.

We take security seriously. All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest. Our team runs regular checks to catch problems early.

New users often ask how to invite teammates. Just go to the team page and enter their email. They will get a link to join right away.

If something breaks, we want to know fast. There is a report button on every page. Our team looks at every report within a few hours.

Exporting your data is easy too. Just click export on the settings page. You will get a file with everything in it.

We also offer a free trial. It lasts fourteen days and needs no credit card. You can upgrade at any point during the trial.
