About Us

Our small team designs and ships quality widgets to customers around the world every single day of the year.

Every widget is inspected twice before it leaves our workshop to make sure it meets our own high standard.

We have been making widgets for over a decade and take great pride in the craft behind every single piece.

Customers can reach our support team by phone or email any day of the week for help with an order.

Shipping is handled by a regional courier that specializes in careful handling of fragile handcrafted goods like ours.

We source our raw materials from a small group of trusted regional suppliers who share our own commitment to quality.

Our workshop sits in a converted warehouse where every bench is arranged for careful, deliberate, unhurried craftsmanship each morning.

New apprentices spend their first six months learning every step of the process before they touch a finished piece.
