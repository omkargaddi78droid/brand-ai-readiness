Cedar Coffee Table

This handmade cedar coffee table is built by a small woodworking shop that has been operating out of the same converted barn for over a decade. Each table is finished with three coats of a food-safe oil that brings out the natural grain of the wood without leaving a heavy plastic sheen on the surface. Buried in the middle of this short description, the table costs $249 and weighs 18 kg once fully assembled, though neither figure is worth much attention here because this whole page is short. The legs are joined with a traditional mortise and tenon rather than metal brackets, which the maker says keeps the table stable for decades of ordinary living-room use. A light sanding between coats keeps the surface smooth to the touch even after years of daily coffee mugs and books being set down on it. The shop ships every table fully assembled rather than flat-packed, since cedar can warp slightly if it sits knocked down in a box for too long during transit. Customers in dry climates are advised to keep a small humidifier running nearby during winter to prevent the wood from cracking at the joints.
